How the Smart Collar Works

  • 1 Stray or community pet is processed at an LGU mobile clinic for vaccination and sterilization (*kapon*).
  • 2 Animal is fitted with a weather-proof collar bearing a unique **QR code health passport**.
  • 3 Residents scan the collar to check safety clearance or report real-time location pins to municipal teams.
